The idea

Navi copies CleanShot X feature for feature, minus the cloud. Every capture (screenshot, scrolling shot, recording, GIF, OCR text) goes to the same place: a corner card first, then the shared history that clipboard items already use. A new Swift package, NaviCapture, holds all of this. It talks to the rest of the app only through NaviCore's Inbox.

Steps

Done Clipboard history

Maccy-style picker at ⇧⌘V, pins, preview card, paste. Shared Inbox with copy counts.

Step 1 Capture core and the corner card

  • Capture area, window, and fullscreen with ScreenCaptureKit (SCScreenshotManager).
  • Freeze the screen first, then select on the frozen image. Crosshair, magnifier, pixel size. Space switches to window mode. Snap to window edges.
  • Window capture with nothing behind it: SCContentFilter(desktopIndependentWindow:) captures one window alone, with its shadow and a clear background.
  • Corner card (CleanShot layout): on hover, the card blurs and shows round Copy and Save buttons in the middle. Corners: Pin (top left), Close (top right), Annotate or Trim (bottom left), and Show in Finder (bottom right, where CleanShot has Cloud). Drag it into any app (a file drag with the PNG). Works on every display.
  • Hide desktop icons: leave Finder's desktop icon windows out of the capture filter, and draw a clean wallpaper behind them. The real desktop stays as it is.
  • Every capture goes into the Inbox and shows up in ⇧⌘V with a thumbnail.

Done when: you can capture any of the 3 ways, then copy, save, or drag the card into Slack. Window shots have no desktop behind them, and hidden icons never show.

Step 2 Annotate editor

  • Arrow, rectangle, filled rectangle, ellipse, line, pencil, highlighter, text, counter, blur, pixelate, spotlight, crop, color picker.
  • Rotate and flip, resize, combine images, undo, and a "drag me" button. Edits stay editable (Navi file format), and export makes a flat PNG.

Done when: you can mark up a capture with every tool, and Done updates the card and the history item.

Step 3 Extras

  • Pin: a floating, always-on-top window. You can change its size and opacity, and lock it so clicks go through it.
  • OCR and QR: the Vision framework (VNRecognizeTextRequest, VNDetectBarcodesRequest). On-device, with the text copied.
  • Self-timer, All-in-one mode (one hotkey, then pick the mode), and quick actions on the card.

Step 4 Scrolling capture and background tool

  • Scrolling capture: grab frames while you scroll, line them up with Vision (VNTranslationalImageRegistrationRequest), and stitch them into one tall image. Vertical and horizontal.
  • Background tool: gradients and your own images, padding, shadow, aspect ratio, auto balance, and saved presets.

Step 5 Screen recording (after talk to type)

  • Record an area, a window, or the full screen with SCStream, and write MP4 (H.264) with AVAssetWriter. Make GIFs with ImageIO first, and maybe gifski (Rust) later.
  • Mic, computer audio (ScreenCaptureKit), camera bubble (AVCaptureSession), and click and keystroke overlays (listen-only CGEventTap).
  • Mute notifications: leave Notification Center's windows out of the capture filter. A notification can still show on screen, but it never gets into the video. If you want, Navi can also turn on a Focus through a Shortcut you install once.
  • Record bar (shows after you pick an area): mic on or off, an options menu (computer audio, camera, clicks, keystrokes, hide icons), the exact width × height you can type in, a fullscreen button, then Record GIF or Record Video.
  • Stop from the red pill in the menu bar. Trim inside the app (AVPlayerView trimming, then export).
  • After that: the Screen Studio-style editor (smart zoom, cursor smoothing, motion blur, backgrounds).

Permissions

  • Screen Recording: needed for all capture (step 1).
  • Accessibility: already granted. It's used for paste.
  • Microphone and Camera: asked the first time you use them (step 5).
  • Input Monitoring: only for the keystroke overlay (step 5).

Decided

  • Hotkeys: Navi borrows ⇧⌘3 and ⇧⌘4 from macOS while it runs. It turns the macOS shortcuts off at launch and puts them back on quit. ⇧⌘5 comes with recording.
  • Save folder: the Desktop.
  • Card auto-close: never. Close with ✕, Copy, Save, ⌘W on hover, or Close All Cards.
